## Full Description

Transform your street photography with **AutoKorrektur** — the fast, privacy-first AI object remover designed to erase unwanted parked cars, vehicles, and urban clutter from your photos!

Whether you are taking architectural shots, urban landscape photos, or real estate listings, unwanted parked cars can ruin a clean frame. AutoKorrektur uses state-of-the-art artificial intelligence (YOLO & MI-GAN) to automatically detect vehicles and seamlessly inpaint the background behind them.

### Key Features:

🚗 **Automated Vehicle Detection**
Instant AI detection of cars, trucks, buses, and motorcycles in your photos.

✨ **Seamless AI Inpainting**
Fills in road surfaces, sidewalks, and building facades with realistic textures.

📱 **Interactive Before/After Split-Slider**
Touch and swipe across your photo to compare the original and car-free result in real time!

📸 **Instagram Comparison Graphic Generator**
Create high-resolution 1:1 Square, 4:5 Portrait, or 9:16 Story comparison graphics with custom "BEFORE" & "AFTER" badges ready to post on social media.

🔒 **100% On-Device & Private**
All processing happens directly on your phone's processor. Zero photos are uploaded to the cloud, guaranteeing complete privacy and offline operation.

⚡ **Batch Processing**
Clean multiple photos at once with automated batch processing.
